How it works

Sign-up takes about five minutes.

  1. Verify you're a studentSign up with your school .edu email and a confirmation code.
  2. Build your profilePick your free times, interests, goals and favorite places to meet.
  3. Meet upWave at matches or join an event, then plan it in the chat.

Built for safety

Meeting new people should feel easy, not risky.

  • 🎓 Students only. Every account is verified with a school .edu email.
  • 🔒 Your email, phone number and ZIP code are never shown to matches.
  • 📍 Chats remind everyone to meet in public places.
  • 🚩 Report or block anyone, any time.
